## Browser-efficiency rules

- Browser automation is for targeted inspection and final verification only.
- Never explore a website broadly unless explicitly requested.
- Prefer direct URLs, HTTP fetching, local files, APIs and deterministic scripts.
- Limit each visual verification to the relevant page and viewport.
- Do not repeatedly capture unchanged pages.
- Stop after two unsuccessful attempts and report the blocker.
- Before browsing, state the exact information or condition being checked.
