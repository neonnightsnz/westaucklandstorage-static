# Stage 8: Owner Questions

**Goal:** a single, answered question bank that feeds every page. Ask once, use
everywhere — this is what stops you inventing facts.

## How to run it

- Send the questions **before** the meeting so the owner can gather specifics.
- Ask in words they use, not marketing language.
- Record answers verbatim. Their phrasing is the site's vocabulary.
- Mark anything they are unsure about as `UNVERIFIED` and follow up.

## Business identity
- [ ] What is the official business name, and what do customers call you?
- [ ] How long have you been trading?
- [ ] What do you do that competitors do not?
- [ ] What kind of work do you want more of? Less of?

## Services
- [ ] What are your core services?
- [ ] What services do people ask for that you do not offer?
- [ ] Do you handle emergencies or after-hours work?
- [ ] What is included in a standard job?
- [ ] What is typically *not* included, that customers assume is?

## Customers
- [ ] Who is your ideal customer?
- [ ] Who is a customer you would rather not attract?
- [ ] What do customers usually say when they first call?
- [ ] What questions do you answer most often on the phone?
- [ ] What worries customers before they book?
- [ ] How do they usually find you?

## Service area
- [ ] Which towns, suburbs or regions do you actually serve?
- [ ] Where will you not travel?
- [ ] Do you charge for travel or callout? Under what conditions?
- [ ] Do you have a physical address customers can visit?

## Trust and proof
- [ ] What licences or registrations do you hold? (get numbers)
- [ ] What certifications or memberships? (get proof and expiry)
- [ ] Any manufacturer or dealer authorisation? (verify before publishing)
- [ ] What guarantees do you offer, in writing?
- [ ] Do you have insurance? (type, if publishable)
- [ ] Do you have reviews, testimonials or project photos I can use?
- [ ] Can I use customer names, or should they stay anonymous?
- [ ] What response time can you actually commit to?

## Conversion and process
- [ ] What is the best way for a customer to reach you?
- [ ] What happens after someone enquires — step by step?
- [ ] Are there times of year you are flat out or quiet?
- [ ] What is the biggest reason enquiries do not turn into jobs?

## Approval
- [ ] Who signs off content?
- [ ] Who signs off photography and testimonials?
- [ ] Who owns the domain, hosting and Google Business Profile?

## Output

A completed question bank, with every answer attributed to the owner and every
uncertainty flagged.
