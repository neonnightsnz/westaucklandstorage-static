# Stage 2: Business Discovery

**Goal:** collect confirmed facts. Everything downstream depends on these being
true — this is the stage that prevents confident copy about services the
business does not actually offer.

## Confirmed facts (owner-verified)

Fill in with the owner. Mark anything unconfirmed as `UNVERIFIED` and do not
publish it.

### Identity
- [ ] Legal business name
- [ ] Trading name(s) used in marketing
- [ ] Business structure (sole trader, company, franchise)
- [ ] Year established
- [ ] Brand tone of voice (trade / professional / friendly / technical)

### Contact and NAP (must match Google Business Profile exactly)
- [ ] Public phone number
- [ ] Email address
- [ ] Physical address (or `SAB` if service-area only)
- [ ] Opening hours (including after-hours / emergency availability)
- [ ] Preferred enquiry method

### Services
- [ ] Core services (the ones that pay the bills)
- [ ] Secondary services
- [ ] Services they do **not** offer (critical — prevents wrong-fit leads)
- [ ] Emergency / after-hours availability, if any
- [ ] Typical job size and price band, if the owner will share

### Service area
- [ ] Towns, suburbs or regions genuinely served
- [ ] Travel or callout policy and any surcharge zones
- [ ] Areas they will **not** travel to

### Credibility
- [ ] Licences and registration numbers
- [ ] Certifications and memberships (with expiry dates)
- [ ] Insurance
- [ ] Warranties or guarantees offered
- [ ] Manufacturer / dealer status (if claimed, verify it)
- [ ] Awards or recognitions

### Capacity and commercial
- [ ] Realistic enquiry response time
- [ ] Booking lead time
- [ ] Seasonality (when the phone rings)
- [ ] Ideal customer vs. unprofitable customer

## Assumption audit

Before moving on, list every assumption you are making about this business.
For each, note how you would verify it. The most expensive SEO mistakes come
from unexamined assumptions — most commonly, that the homepage represents the
site, or that the marketing team's description of the customer matches what
customers actually ask.

## Output

A completed, owner-confirmed fact sheet. Anything still unverified is flagged
and excluded from publishing.
