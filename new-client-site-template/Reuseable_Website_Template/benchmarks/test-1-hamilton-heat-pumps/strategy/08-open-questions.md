# 08 — Open Questions for the Owner

Eleven questions survived research. Six block work.

## Blocking — cannot proceed without these

**Q1. How many Google reviews do you have, and what is your current rating?**
Your top competitor has 468. You are not listed in either Hamilton ranking,
which suggests the review record is thin or not being surfaced.
*Blocks: the entire trust layer, which is priority 1.*

**Q2. Do you hold Mitsubishi Electric or Daikin dealer / certified-installer
status?** You named both brands but your site claims neither. If you hold it,
this is your strongest unused credential.
*Blocks: the two brand pages.*

**Q3. What workmanship warranty do you offer?** Three competitors publish a
multi-year warranty. A warranty is checkable; "customer satisfaction" is not.
*Blocks: all service pages.*

**Q4. Which towns and suburbs do you genuinely serve?** Your site has Location
pages for Hamilton suburbs. I need the real list, including whether you cross
into Waikato towns like Cambridge, Te Awamutu or Huntly.
*Blocks: Location-page audit and any new Location pages.*

**Q5. What do you actually charge?** Competitors publish ranges ($2,500–$4,500
single split). A range removes the biggest friction point in this market.
*Blocks: the cost article, which is priority 1.*

**Q6. Are you a registered Warmer Kiwi Homes provider?** No competitor leads
with this. It is the clearest open position, but only if you can deliver it.
*Blocks: the Warmer Kiwi Homes page.*

## Important — affects scope

**Q7. Are all ten services on your site real?** The list includes refrigerant
leak detection and thermostat upgrades, which need specific equipment. Some may
have been written for the site rather than reflecting what you do.
*Affects: whether to keep or cut service pages.*

**Q8. What is your actual response time?** I will not publish a promise you have
not committed to.

**Q9. Do you want commercial work?** You mentioned small commercial jobs. It is a
different buyer with a different sales cycle. Worth building only if wanted.

**Q10. What is the biggest reason quotes do not turn into jobs?** Price, lead
time, or losing to a competitor — the answer changes what we fix first.

## Nice to know

**Q11. How did the current website come to look templated?** If it was built by a
lead-generation or directory service, you may not fully control it, and you may
not own the domain. **Check this before investing in content.**

## What I did not ask, and why

- **The business name, phone and address** — found them in research.
- **Whether heat pumps are the core business** — no evidence of anything else.
- **The brands offered** — you told me.
- **Hamilton being the main market** — confirmed by the site and the brief.
