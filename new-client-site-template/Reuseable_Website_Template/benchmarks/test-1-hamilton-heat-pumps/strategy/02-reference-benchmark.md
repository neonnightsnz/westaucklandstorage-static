# 02 — Reference Benchmark

**Method:** top5.nz and 10best.co.nz both publish ranked Hamilton heat pump
installer lists. top5.nz states it assessed **19 installers** and ranked the top
5 on public review record; the 10 it lists hold **1,245 Google reviews**
between them. Neither list includes Hamilton Heat Pumps.

## Why competitors win

1. **Review volume and recency.** 468 vs unknown. top5.nz ranks explicitly on
   rating quality, review volume and recency.
2. **Named credentials.** "Authorised Daikin Specialist Dealer" and "Mitsubishi
   Electric Authorised Service Centre" are checkable claims. Hamilton Heat Pumps
   states the brands but claims no dealer status.
3. **Published warranties.** Three competitors publish a multi-year workmanship
   warranty. A warranty is a concrete risk-reversal; "customer satisfaction" is
   not.
4. **Published pricing.** Alectrics publishes "$2,500 to $4,500 fully installed".
   Hamilton Heat Pumps answers the pricing question with "it varies" and defers
   to a quote.
5. **A defined process.** Aeon Energy publishes a 3-step process with timings
   (install within 2 weeks of quote; ~4 hours on the day; follow-up call within
   a month). Hamilton Heat Pumps has no process.
6. **Local knowledge.** Aircon Group Waikato leads with "the unique climate
   challenges in Hamilton and the wider Waikato". Hamilton Heat Pumps says
   nothing about local conditions.

## Patterns worth adapting

- **A published install process with timescales** (Aeon). Cheap to produce, high
  trust value, and it directly answers "what happens next?".
- **A named workmanship warranty** (Superheat, Aircon Group, Hamilton Aircons).
- **A price band** (Alectrics) — even a range removes the biggest friction point.
- **Local climate framing** (Aircon Group) — Hamilton frosts and Waikato humidity
  are real, specific, and currently unclaimed in a meaningful way.

## Patterns to avoid

- **Templated filler** — the current site's own weakness; do not replicate it.
- **Ten-service laundry lists** — dilutes focus and invites wrong-fit enquiries.

## The gap we can own

Not "cheapest" and not "most reviews" — those are taken. The open position is
**the Warmer Kiwi Homes / subsidy specialists**: businesses that clearly explain
eligibility, what the grant covers, what the customer actually pays, and the
wait times. No competitor in this set is leading with it, and it targets exactly
the customer the brief describes (residential, Hamilton/Waikato).
