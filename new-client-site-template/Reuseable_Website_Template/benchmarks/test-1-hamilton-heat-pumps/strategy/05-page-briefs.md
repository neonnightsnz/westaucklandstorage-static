- **Intent:** Local service (transactional)
- **Target query:** heat pump installation hamilton
- **Words:** 800
- **Primary CTA:** Get a free quote

**Sections:** what's included · system types (single split, multi-head, ducted)
· indicative price band · the install process with timescales · warranty terms ·
brands offered · 4–6 FAQs · CTA.

**Proof needed:** warranty terms · price band · a real installation photo · a

process, Alectrics' pricing, Superheat's warranty) and closes the three biggest
conversion gaps at once.

## Warmer Kiwi Homes — `/services/warmer-kiwi-homes/`

- **Intent:** Commercial investigation (high value)
- **Target query:** warmer kiwi homes heat pump / heat pump grant hamilton
- **Words:** 800
- **Primary CTA:** Check if you qualify

**Must include:** what the programme is · eligibility (Community Services Card,
SuperGold Combo, or low-income area) · **up to 90% covered, capped at $3,450
incl. GST** · **high-wall units only** — ducted and floor-mount are not funded ·
what the customer pays in the balance · how to apply · that this business can
handle it · CTA.

**Why this page:** no competitor in the ranking set leads with it. It targets
exactly the customer described in the brief, and it is the single clearest
differentiator available.

**Must verify:** that this business is an approved Warmer Kiwi Homes provider
**before** publishing anything implying eligibility to deliver the grant.

## Cost guide — `/blog/heat-pump-installation-cost/` (priority 1)

- **Intent:** Commercial investigation
- **Target query:** heat pump cost nz / heat pump installation cost hamilton
- **Words:** 1,500
- **Sections:** price bands by system type ($2,000–$4,500 single split;
  $5,000–$12,000+ multi-head; up to $20,000 ducted) · what drives cost (capacity,
  brand, install complexity, electrical work) · Warmer Kiwi Homes offset ·
  running costs · when to replace vs repair · CTA to quote.

**Why:** "how much" is the highest-friction question in this market, competitors
publish ranges, and this site currently answers it with "it varies".
