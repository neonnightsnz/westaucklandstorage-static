## The single biggest gap

**Reviews.** The competitor ranked #1 has 468; the ten ranked businesses hold
1,245 between them. If this business has reviews that are not being surfaced,
that is the cheapest available win. If it genuinely has very few, review
generation becomes the first priority, ahead of any website work.

Review velocity matters more than total volume: a profile with no new reviews
for weeks loses ground.

## E-E-A-T assessment

- **Authoritativeness** — absent from both local rankings; no credentials. *Weak.*
- **Trustworthiness** — no address on the homepage, no warranty, no pricing,

All four need work. Trustworthiness is the fastest to fix and affects conversion
immediately.

## Review compliance, do not get this wrong

- **Review gating is prohibited.** No filtering customers by sentiment before
  asking, and no asking only happy customers.
- **Google fake-engagement policy** prohibits incentivised or fabricated reviews.
- **FTC Consumer Review Rule** (effective 21 Oct 2024): penalties up to
  **$53,088 per violation** in the US.
- The **NZ Fair Trading Act** applies to misleading claims on the same principle.
- Ask **every** customer, the same way, at job completion.
- Never write a review on a customer's behalf.
