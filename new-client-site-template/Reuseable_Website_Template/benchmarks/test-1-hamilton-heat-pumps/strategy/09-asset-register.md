## Photography direction

Real work photos beat stock for a trade business and double as E-E-A-T
evidence. Priority shots:

1. An installer working on an outdoor unit (hero for installation)
2. Indoor unit on a wall, newly installed
3. A van or team at a job
4. Close-up of a clean filter or coil (servicing)
5. Before/after of a cramped install if one exists

**Do not** use stock images, and never imply work the business has not done.

## Technical requirements

- [ ] Descriptive alt text, 10–125 characters, on every non-decorative image
- [ ] Decorative images use empty alt
- [ ] Descriptive filenames (heat-pump-installation-hamilton.webp)
- [ ] Compressed, WebP/AVIF, correctly sized
- [ ] Explicit width/height to prevent layout shift
- [ ] Hero image preloaded, not lazy-loaded

## Reputational risk

If the site is operated by a lead-generation or directory service, the owner may
not own the domain or the content. **Establish ownership before commissioning any
photography or copy.** See Q11.
