# 10 — Approval and Review

## Owner must confirm in writing before publication

- [ ] Business name, phone, address, opening hours
- [ ] Service area — the complete list of suburbs and towns served
- [ ] Which of the ten advertised services are real
- [ ] Dealer / certified-installer status (Mitsubishi Electric, Daikin)
- [ ] Licences and registrations, with numbers
- [ ] Workmanship warranty — exact wording and duration
- [ ] Insurance
- [ ] Years trading
- [ ] Review count and rating as at publication date
- [ ] Price band, or confirmation that none will be published
- [ ] Response time, or confirmation that no promise will be made
- [ ] Callout fee structure
- [ ] Warmer Kiwi Homes provider status
- [ ] Testimonials and customer names, with permission
- [ ] Photos — ownership and permission

## Approval workflow

1. Draft written against the Stage 5 brief
2. Technical review (correct terminology, no invented specs)
3. Owner review of all claims, names, numbers, locations
4. Metadata review
5. Approval recorded with date
6. Publish
