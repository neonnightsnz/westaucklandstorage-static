Good to know

Questions,
answered.

Where is West Auckland Storage?+

We’re at Span Farm Boat Yard, 20 Akatea Road, Glendene, Waitakere 0602 — close to the communities we serve across West Auckland.

What can I store?+

We store boats, caravans, trailers, cars, 4WDs, utes, trucks, motorbikes, kayaks and other recreational gear. Get in touch with the size and type of item you have and we’ll talk through the options.

Can I store something short-term?+

Yes. We can discuss short stays, winter storage and longer-term arrangements. We keep the setup straightforward and invoice monthly.

Is slipway access available?+

Slipway access is available for smaller trailer boats when operational. Ask us about current access and suitability before you arrive so we can give you the right answer.
